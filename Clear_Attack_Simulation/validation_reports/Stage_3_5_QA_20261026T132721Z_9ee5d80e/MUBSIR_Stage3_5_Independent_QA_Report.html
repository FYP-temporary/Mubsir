11. Limitations and Remaining Responsibilities

  1. The simulated injections modify aggregated event counts. Individual synthetic event IDs, event timestamps, and payload transfers were not generated.
  2. The 42,095 injected events represent additional activity counts, not verified exfiltrated bytes.
  3. The historical baseline was verified against pre-2011 aggregated User-Day data; raw event-level reconstruction remains outside this audit scope.
  4. Temporal prefix tests independently checked 9 users and 1,620 feature comparisons; this does not establish the absence of leakage in future machine-learning train/test splitting.
  5. The five mutation tests demonstrate detection of the five tested errors only, not all possible data-corruption or semantic errors.
  6. High-stealth channel-days may exceed historical P95. These exceedances are documented and are not silently removed.
  7. An Off or Outside Schedule label means no synthetic injection was made on that day. It does not independently prove benign intent.
  8. Feature correctness and statistical progression do not establish machine-learning detection performance or real-world attack realism.

12. Final Conclusion

Overall QA Status: PASS. The simulated attack quantities, approved schedules, 115-column feature data, historical baseline statistics, and three Ground Truth tables passed the specified independent mathematical and consistency checks.

The validated datasets are ready for controlled ML experimentation. Model training, leakage-safe evaluation, exfiltration-class performance, and external realism assessment remain separate tasks.
